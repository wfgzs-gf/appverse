Appverse demo artwork
=====================

All images in this directory were generated specifically for the Appverse
WordPress theme with Python/Pillow. They are released under CC0 1.0
(public domain dedication): you may use, modify and redistribute them,
including in commercial projects, without attribution.

Contents:
- icons/     12 app icons + default fallback (256x256 PNG)
- banners/   3 home carousel backgrounds (1200x560 JPEG)
- avatars/   4 user avatars (256x256 PNG)
- screens/   3 generic app screenshot mock-ups (720x1280 PNG)
- manifest.json  mapping of files, titles and dominant colors

No real product logos, screenshots or copyrighted artwork are included.
